New to catchball?

Easy to learn. Fun from the first session.

Catchball is a team sport in which players catch, pass and throw the ball over a net. Two teams work together to keep the ball off the ground on their side and land it in the other team’s court.

It combines movement, coordination and teamwork with the enjoyment of playing together. At CatchLux, we explain the basics and help you learn as you play.

No prior catchball experience needed.
