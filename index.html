About CatchLux

CatchLux is a friendly women's catchball community based in Luxembourg.

Our goal is simple: bring women together to play regularly, have fun, stay active, and build friendships.

We are not a business and operate on a not-for-profit basis. The participation fee only covers the cost of renting the sports hall.

Welcome Message

Hi everyone! 💙❤️

We're so excited to launch CatchLux and build a friendly women's catchball community here in Luxembourg!

Our goal is simple: to create a fun group that can meet and play together on a regular basis. We're not a business and operate on a not-for-profit basis—we're simply covering the cost of renting the sports hall.

We're excited to begin our regular training sessions starting in September 2026, and we'd love for you to join us!

To reserve your spot, please send the €12 participation fee via Payconiq. The fee only covers the hall rental. Once payment is received, your place is confirmed.

Places are limited, so we recommend registering early to secure your spot.

If you have any questions or would like to know more about catchball, feel free to message us anytime.

We can't wait to meet you all! 🏐

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience?

No! Beginners are very welcome.

What should I bring?

Sports clothes, indoor shoes, water, and a smile.

Who can join?

Any woman who would like to play catchball, stay active, and be part of our community is welcome.

How do I register?

Join our WhatsApp group or contact us directly. Your place is confirmed after receiving the €12 participation fee.
