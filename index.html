New to catchball?

Easy to learn. Fun from the first session.

Catchball is a team sport played over a net. It feels a little like volleyball, but instead of volleying the ball, players catch and throw it.

That makes it accessible for beginners while still giving you plenty of movement, teamwork and game play.

No prior catchball experience needed.
